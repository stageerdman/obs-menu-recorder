## Recently fixed, needs confirmation on real hardware/connection
- [ ] OneDrive upload timeouts (fixed a4a9947, pushed 2026-09-23): chunk PUTs went
      through URLSession.shared, whose default 60s timeoutIntervalForRequest failed any
      8 MiB chunk that couldn't complete in a minute (upstream slower than ~1.1 Mbps, or
      a stall). OneDriveClient now uses a dedicated URLSession (300s request timeout, 6h
      resource ceiling). Confirm a large recording that previously timed out now uploads
      to completion. If it STILL times out, the upstream can't push 8 MiB even in 300s —
      next lever is lowering OneDriveClient.chunkSize (currently 8 MiB).

## Known upstream limitations (no in-app fix)
- OBS quit-time crash (copy_audio_data segfault) — RecBar never quits OBS as a result.
- Camera "stuck open" after a real recording: RemoveInput can report success while the
  AVCaptureSession never tears down; only a manual OBS quit/relaunch clears it.
